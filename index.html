Pipeline

  
From Microsoft 365 to an answer

  
The layer your AI teacher and your document assistant are waiting for, deployed on your own Jelastic and handed over with documentation.

  

    
  

  

    
1
Sync with zero duplicates
Microsoft Graph delta returns new, changed and deleted items. We track by item id, not by name, so a renamed folder never duplicates. A single delta batch can mention one file several times and only the last record counts. When a token expires Graph answers 410 and we run a clean resync. A deleted file is also removed from pgvector, so the assistant never answers from a document you removed.

    
2
Two level OCR, by measurement
Clean pages stay on local OCR, which you saw read Kazakh at 96.4 above. Tables, stamps and weak scans go to a vision model. Published research on Kazakh scripts shows blanket LLM correction of Cyrillic Kazakh adds little and can hallucinate, so official text is never rewritten silently. DOCX files and PDFs that already carry a text layer skip OCR completely, which is faster and exact. Pages the gate cannot trust go to Review Required, for human eyes.

    
3
Structure into pgvector
Headings, sections, pages and tables survive extraction, then the text splits into chunks with embeddings in PostgreSQL with pgvector. A question returns the exact passage with its page and section. That is the loop your AI teacher needs, a syllabus goes into a folder and becomes answerable knowledge without anyone uploading it by hand.

    
4
Deploys on your Jelastic
The stock PostgreSQL template does not include pgvector, so we ship a prepared image and a one click JPS manifest. API, workers and the database run as separate layers, and the OCR worker layer scales out when a large batch arrives. Your team can redeploy the whole thing without us.

  







  
Stack and models

  
The exact models we propose

  
You asked which OCR and vision models we would use. Here they are by name, with the reason for each and what they cost you in speed.

  

    
1
Local OCR
Tesseract 5 with the kaz and rus language data, the same engine you saw read your Kazakh page at 96.4 on this site. It runs inside your own environment, so standard pages never leave your infrastructure and cost nothing per page.

    
2
Vision model
Two options, both behind a config switch. Gemini 2.5 Flash for speed and price, published research shows it performs best on Kazakh post OCR correction. Or Qwen2.5 VL running on your own server, so nothing leaves Miras at all. You choose, and you can change it later without touching code.

    
3
Embeddings
BGE M3 or multilingual E5 large, both open weight and both strong on Kazakh and Russian, both able to run on your own hardware. Vectors go into PostgreSQL with pgvector using an HNSW index, so search stays fast as the archive grows.

    
4
Infrastructure
Python with FastAPI for the API, Celery with Redis for the queues and retries, Docker layers on your Jelastic, and a prepared PostgreSQL image because the stock Jelastic template does not carry pgvector. Your existing Laravel platform stays untouched and simply calls this service.

  

  

    Throughput
    One OCR worker handles roughly 700 to 900 scanned pages an hour. The worker layer scales sideways on Jelastic, so four workers move a few thousand pages an hour and an archive of ten thousand pages is a matter of hours, not weeks. Vision pages are slower and stay a minority by design.

Corporate data security

  
Read only, least privilege, your consent

  

    
Read only scopes
The connection asks only for Files.Read.All and Sites.Read.All. Nothing is ever written back to your OneDrive or SharePoint.

    
Your admin approves
Access starts only after your own tenant administrator grants consent, and can be revoked in your admin center at any minute.

    
Data stays with you
Documents, extracted text and embeddings live inside your Jelastic environment. The LLM provider is a config switch and can point to a provider you approve.

    
This page asks for nothing
The live test above runs inside your browser. No account, no upload, no tracking. We request no access at this stage.
